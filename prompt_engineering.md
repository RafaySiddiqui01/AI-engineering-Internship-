## Task
Summarize a news article about Artificial Intelligence.


## Prompt Version 1 – Basic Prompt

**Prompt:**
Summarize the following article.


### Result
- Produced a general summary.
- Missed some important details.


## Prompt Version 2 – Bullet Point Summary

**Prompt:**
Summarize the following article in 5 concise bullet points highlighting the key information.

### Result
- Easier to read.
- Captured the main ideas clearly.
- Better than Version 1.

## Prompt Version 3 – Executive Summary

**Prompt:**

You are an AI research assistant.
Read the article and provide:
1. A summary in under 100 words.
2. Three key takeaways.
3. The overall tone of the article.

### Result
- Well-structured output.
- Included useful insights.
- More informative than previous prompts.

## Prompt Version 4 – Student-Friendly Summary

**Prompt:**

Explain the article as if you are teaching a first-year university student.
Use simple language and include an example if helpful.


### Result
- Very easy to understand.
- Simplified complex concepts.
- Best for educational purposes.
